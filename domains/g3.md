# Grade 3 and 5 reading

37 learnings that hold in Grade 3 and 5 reading work rather than everywhere. The universal ones are in [PERFECT-QUESTION.md](../PERFECT-QUESTION.md) and [GENERATING.md](../GENERATING.md).

## What a perfect question is, here

### Stimulus: the text, document or source a question rests on

- Teach poetry, drama and classic tales on texts inside the knowledge units, not in separate genre units, and check that every form the standards name appears. [record](../groups/the-text.md#literary-forms-inside-the-knowledge-units)

### Feedback: what a student is told after answering

- When a learner games a task, name the behaviour, never the learner, and explain any penalty at the end with the way back, never as a live scold. [record](../groups/what-a-student-is-told.md#name-the-behaviour-not-the-child)
- Give points once at the finish, for whole focused minutes and a passed checkpoint, never for each correct answer. [record](../groups/what-a-student-is-told.md#points-for-focused-time-not-answers)
- When a learner's written answer misses the target, show a short list of what the answer is not, drawn from the lesson's own knowledge bank. [record](../groups/what-a-student-is-told.md#show-what-the-answer-is-not)

### Hints: help before the answer is given

- During word reading, prompt a stuck reader with the letters and sounds rather than 'what would make sense', and during comprehension, prompt for meaning and reasoning. [record](../groups/what-a-student-is-told.md#code-prompts-for-decoding-meaning-prompts-for-comprehension)

### Demand: the thinking a task requires

- When an evidence-selection item stays a locate item, make it demanding: the sentence must meet two conditions at once, or be chosen for the role it plays. [record](../groups/writing-questions.md#make-a-locate-item-demanding)

### Alignment: fit to the objective, unit and what was taught

- Tag a reading item with the operation it actually demands, never with the text-complexity standard as if that band were the skill tested. [record](../groups/writing-questions.md#the-complexity-standard-is-not-a-skill)

### Sequence: order and spacing across a lesson or course

- Organise a unit and a course around a few fixed concepts that every lesson returns to and adds to, and record which of them each lesson draws on. [record](../groups/how-people-learn.md#a-few-concepts-every-lesson-returns-to)
- Where a writing rubric gives an undeveloped response no credit for conventions, teach learners to develop and organise ideas before polishing conventions. [record](../groups/how-people-learn.md#develop-ideas-before-polishing-conventions)
- Keep every component in every lesson and vary only its time, by lesson type and learner, with floors: support shrinks but never vanishes, and retrieval keeps about a tenth. [record](../groups/how-people-learn.md#fixed-components-flexible-time)
- Let a lesson depend on at most one hard prerequisite, and treat needing more as a sign the lesson is oversized and should be split, not given more gates. [record](../groups/how-people-learn.md#one-hard-prerequisite-per-lesson)
- When learners arrive with no usable background in a unit's domain, make its first lesson an ungated knowledge build, say so, and start comprehension gates in the next lesson. [record](../groups/how-people-learn.md#open-a-cold-domain-with-an-ungated-build)
- Remove a scaffold outright at a grade boundary, rather than fading it, when the skill it supports is expected as a matter of course from then on. [record](../groups/how-people-learn.md#some-scaffolds-end-at-a-grade-boundary)
- Teach a new multi-syllable word in a fixed order: hear it in syllables, break it into meaningful parts where it has them, give its definition, then an example. [record](../groups/how-people-learn.md#syllables-word-parts-definition-then-example)
- Narrate a long text read in parts: open each part with a short line on what to watch for, review it afterwards, and summarise at the end. [record](../groups/what-a-student-is-told.md#narrate-between-the-sections-of-a-long-text)
- Once a procedure has been modelled in full, let learners skip its earlier modelled steps, but always play the step that models the written answer in full. [record](../groups/how-people-learn.md#never-skip-the-modelled-write-up)

### Wording: the language a student reads

- Keep a word card short: a one-sentence everyday definition of at most 20 words, and a short example and non-example, since each extra clause weakens the contrast. [record](../groups/the-text.md#a-word-card-short-enough-to-hold)
- Keep adult punctuation and shorthand, such as em dashes, semicolons, any brackets, slash compounds and all-caps, out of young readers' text outside quoted source, and check it in code. [record](../groups/what-a-student-is-told.md#no-adult-punctuation-for-young-readers)
- Keep the words a learner reads just before acting to a short plain instruction of at most 14 words, and put any framing in the guide's narration. [record](../groups/what-a-student-is-told.md#plain-short-text-where-the-learner-acts)
- Once learners decode fluently, as expected from grade 3, leave phonics out of vocabulary teaching and break taught words into the parts that carry meaning. [record](../groups/how-people-learn.md#teach-word-parts-by-meaning-not-sound)

### Integrity: leaks, cues, gaming and truthfulness

- A target the learner hunts for to locate evidence, such as a word, must pick out at most two of the paragraphs searched, since a target found everywhere teaches nothing. [record](../groups/writing-questions.md#a-hunt-target-in-few-paragraphs)

### Media: pictures, sound and film

- On a scored reading test, read aloud nothing whose reading is assessed, such as the passage or answer options, since listening replaces reading and changes what is measured. [record](../groups/pictures-sound-film.md#no-read-aloud-where-reading-is-tested)
- In an early-reading course, voice every corrective and confirming feedback line, because the learner who most needs the correction is the one least able to read it. [record](../groups/pictures-sound-film.md#voice-the-feedback-for-early-readers)

### Practice: how learners practise and retry

- Have learners say each newly taught term aloud as unscored practice, since clicking a word doesn't build command of it. [record](../groups/how-people-learn.md#say-each-new-term-aloud)
- Keep a comprehension exchange on one idea for about five turns, each reply building on the learner's answer rather than only judging it, and measure turns per conversation. [record](../groups/how-people-learn.md#about-five-turns-on-one-idea)
- Don't ask young learners for the same production twice in one word activity, aloud and then typed; let writing happen once, where it must carry evidence and meaning. [record](../groups/how-people-learn.md#dont-ask-for-the-same-production-twice)
- Where a course pays points both for teaching steps and for graded questions, keep the two apart, so a poor quiz never cancels what was earned while learning. [record](../groups/what-a-student-is-told.md#teaching-points-apart-from-quiz-points)

## How to generate them, here

### 1. Ground: fix the objective, the scope and the facts before writing

- Trigger a full worked model the first time each learner meets each procedure at each stage, tracked per learner rather than by date, and cost that trigger before building. [record](../groups/making-and-reviewing.md#cost-a-per-learner-model-trigger)
- Cost every path through an adaptive lesson against its accepted time window before building, and when shedding optional parts can't fit a path, change the scheme, not the protected parts. [record](../groups/making-and-reviewing.md#cost-every-path-against-the-time-window)

### 2. Write: draft the stimulus, question, options, guide and feedback

- Cut a word to be sounded out into two to four chunks, each sayable alone and as it sounds in the word, merging any lone schwa into a neighbour. [record](../groups/pictures-sound-film.md#cut-a-sound-out-into-sayable-chunks)

### 3. Settle: check in code everything code can decide

- To catch a question that needs a story detail not yet read, track only words concentrated in at most two of the collection's texts, measured from the texts themselves. [record](../groups/making-and-reviewing.md#find-story-words-by-their-concentration)
- In science-bearing text, fail any listed misconception phrase even inside a negation, a rejected alternative or a correction, and rewrite it before publication. [record](../groups/making-and-reviewing.md#misconception-phrases-fail-even-when-negated)

### 6. Measure: measure quality and learning the same way each time

- For early readers, measure oral reading rate as well as accuracy, since rate tracks standardized reading scores more closely, and a reader can be accurate yet slow. [record](../groups/making-and-reviewing.md#measure-early-readers-rate-not-only-accuracy)
