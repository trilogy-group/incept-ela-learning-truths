## Full evidence library


## 1. Ground: fix the objective, the scope and the facts before writing

- Tag items against a closed, versioned list of thinking operations kept apart from format and knowledge labels, so each item is labelled by the work it demands. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#a-closed-list-of-thinking-operations)
- Treat a teaching principle, or a question generated from one, as evidence for a rule: before it drives generation, state its conditions, effect, scope, precedence, exceptions, conflicts and tests. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#a-principle-is-evidence-not-a-rule)
- Before recording narration, agree one pronunciation for every name, number and taught term, write it into the voice direction, and check every clip against it by listening. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#agree-pronunciations-before-recording)
- Give every task's writer the lesson's one question, and point each task that can serve it at what bears on it, preferring the lines a judge found help answer it. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#aim-every-task-at-the-lesson-question)
- Ask a writer only for the specifics a task needs, taken from a verified record, since every name, date or figure it recalls is a chance to invent one. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#ask-only-for-the-specifics-needed)
- Before generating from a source pool or declaring a topic short of material, audit what the pool really holds for each curriculum topic, counting only after normalising its labels. [record](groups/the-text.md#audit-a-source-pools-coverage)
- Mark data borrowed from another group of students, such as another grade's weak skills, as a placeholder, never as measured for the students in hand. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#borrowed-data-stays-a-placeholder)
- Build and check generated items and marking guides against the exam's official released items, scoring guidelines and scored sample answers wherever they exist. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#build-and-check-against-released-exams)
- Build each concept thread backward from a demanding end point, keeping the concept constant and raising the difficulty, so a later stage on the same content always asks for more. [record](groups/how-people-learn.md#build-each-thread-backward)
- Where a rule can be built into what a model is given, build it in rather than rely on a check to catch the breach. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#build-the-rule-in)
- When content links two facts, check that the stated kind and direction of the link hold, not only that the facts are related. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#check-a-links-kind-and-direction)
- Check a model's labels of which facts are essential against people's judgment before they decide what is taught or tested. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#check-a-models-importance-labels)
- Before adopting or generating from curriculum sources, compare candidates item by item on content and check their facts and labels against the official framework, because derived copies shift labels. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#check-curriculum-records-against-the-framework)
- Check every scope label against the official framework before an item is made or judged, pass it explicitly and unchanged through every step, and confirm how the judge reads it. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#check-the-scope-labels-first)
- Check the sources a task rests on before building or grading it, for the right period, real text and distinct documents counted by content. [record](groups/the-text.md#check-the-sources-before-the-task)
- Choose core content by how many independent sources include it, with a fixed inclusion threshold. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#choose-core-content-by-consensus)
- Choose a unit's texts before its questions, each with a stated role in building knowledge of its topic, so that no text could be swapped out without changing the unit. [record](groups/the-text.md#choose-the-texts-before-the-questions)
- Choose what knowledge to teach by its leverage (how often later texts and tests draw on it, what builds on it and how widely it transfers). [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#choose-what-to-teach-by-its-leverage)
- Before a study becomes a design rule, check its learners, task and contrast, say how strong its evidence is, and read its effect size as a direction only. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#claim-no-more-than-the-evidence)
- Clear the rights to every text, clip and image before building on it, since viewing rights don't cover excerpts and a withdrawn text takes everything built on it. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#clear-the-rights-before-building)
- When an objective or standard spans several cases or elements, list them in the plan and give each question a different one, since material missing any one fails alignment. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#cover-every-case-the-objective-spans)
- Give a judge only curriculum data from a real source, and where the source has nothing, leave the field empty rather than have a model fill it. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#curriculum-context-from-real-sources-only)
- Cut each text into units a question can point at, a spoken line with its speaker, a verse line or a sentence, and never count stage directions as evidence. [record](groups/the-text.md#cut-the-text-into-units-to-point-at)
- Before judging a course or assessment, declare its purpose (teaching, practice, mastery gate, diagnostic), judge it by that alone, and set pass marks only on checks that gate progress. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#declare-what-each-assessment-is-for)
- Define each difficulty level per skill and check every label against that skill's own definition, because one global rule for easy or hard mislabels items. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#define-difficulty-per-skill)
- Before writing a scored item, state what it is evidence for: the knowledge it targets, the claim a right answer supports and the evidence that counts. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#design-the-evidence-before-the-item)
- Design what students will see, hear, examine and think about before the interface, and make the interface serve that experience rather than varying layouts without it. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#design-the-experience-before-the-interface)
- Define a standard's difficulty levels so they don't overlap, and check that every learning objective still maps to at least one of them. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#difficulty-levels-that-dont-overlap)
- When a content rule can be read two ways, add a one-line disambiguation and an example of what it does and doesn't forbid. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#disambiguate-a-rule-that-reads-two-ways)
- Don't build a model's examples or training data from its own output; draw them from real material outside it. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#dont-build-on-the-models-own-output)
- Drop an item whose fact can't be traced to a cited source rather than patching the gap with a generated claim. [record](groups/the-text.md#drop-dont-patch)
- Before an activity goes in, write its rationale: the knowledge and performance it targets, the failure it guards against, why this activity, and when its support comes off. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#every-activity-states-its-rationale)
- Cut every excerpt students analyse as a coherent span re-extracted from the original document, record its exact boundary, and reject scanned-text fragments, added modern text, arbitrary cuts and truncations. [record](groups/the-text.md#excerpt-a-coherent-span-boundary-recorded)
- Fetch an item's sources and facts by its exact topic or objective, not its unit, and give the writer only the few that match, since other topics' facts blend in. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#few-facts-matched-to-the-exact-topic)
- Fit a test's floor and ceiling, and any items added to an adaptive bank, to where each grade's students sit, not to their average, which can hide either tail. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#fit-a-tests-range-to-the-tails)
- Fix in advance the order in which a time-boxed lesson's parts give way for a slow learner: enrichment and untested items first, never what a later part depends on. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#fix-in-advance-what-gives-way-first)
- When a text presupposes an idea no knowledge-graph node holds, flag the gap rather than force it onto the nearest node, since no check of the graph can see it. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#flag-the-node-the-graph-lacks)
- Before borrowing standards, texts or materials across school systems, check what each year or grade label means there, since the same number can name different ages. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#grade-labels-differ-between-school-systems)
- Base a reference video's style analysis on timestamped frames actually inspected, with observation kept apart from inference; with few frames narrow the claims, and with none build nothing style-specific. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#ground-a-style-study-in-inspected-frames)
- List the misconceptions each unit must guard against, and screen every text carrying a subject's content for them, even outside that subject's course, by meaning as well as phrase. [record](groups/the-text.md#guard-each-unit-against-misconceptions)
- Identify a standard by its full place in the curriculum, not by its code alone, and return every match, because one code can name several standards. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#identify-a-standard-by-its-full-place)
- Take each unit's and lesson's identity and place from the curriculum map, confirm it before authoring, check the content against it before submission, and never fix a mismatch by renaming. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#identity-and-place-from-the-curriculum-map)
- Keep a ledger of the topics reserved for cold transfer tests, block any generation prompt or exemplar that names one, and retire each topic once it is served. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#keep-cold-test-topics-out-of-prompts)
- When similar standards or sources are merged into one shared unit, keep each one's specific detail inside it, because a merged abstract description is too vague to drive lessons. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#keep-each-sources-detail-when-merging)
- Keep learning objectives and cited facts as two levels of a single store, since either source alone can lose much of what the other holds. [record](groups/the-text.md#keep-objectives-and-cited-facts)
- Keep pipeline and data-format instructions out of a curriculum's descriptions, and don't remove a real constraint from a standard unless it is kept somewhere else. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#keep-standards-free-of-pipeline-details)
- Set a written response's target length from the measured length of top-scoring released answers, not from the size of the answer box. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#length-targets-from-top-scoring-answers)
- Treat a course's lesson counts as forecasts to test on a timed pilot unit, and when the material needs more time, re-plan the calendar rather than cut content or teaching. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#lesson-counts-are-forecasts-to-test)
- Grade a concept only on features every authoritative source treats as defining, teaching usual ones as typical, and never build a wrong option on a sense any source accepts. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#let-the-sources-fix-what-a-definition-grades)
- Choose topics or texts by an open score whose factors, weights and totals anyone can recompute, and let a person override it only with a recorded reason. [record](groups/the-text.md#make-a-scored-selection-recomputable)
- Map every fact taken from a source to the part of the curriculum it serves, and drop or review the ones that fit none. [record](groups/the-text.md#map-every-fact-to-the-curriculum)
- Before teaching a concept, write down its end-of-course target, each known misconception with its persistence, cost and origin, when each framework teaches it, and the riskiest points in the sequence. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#map-the-misconceptions-before-teaching)
- Find and match curriculum data, lesson parts and source texts by their content and structure, never by identifiers or titles, which drift or shift wholesale. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#match-curriculum-data-by-content)
- When a course must cover a required list of texts, count a match only for the text itself, and record another edition or a merely related topic apart from it. [record](groups/the-text.md#match-the-required-text-itself)
- Break each standard into components that each name one point of failure, the error a student makes when it fails, and the kind of knowledge it depends on. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#name-each-skills-point-of-failure)
- A block meant for any text holds nothing made for one text, such as a stored film, pictures or labels, unless each text gets its own, checked for fit. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#nothing-tied-to-one-text)
- Give every standard the same core description (key concepts, learning objectives, assessment boundaries, common misconceptions, and easy, medium and hard definitions) in one versioned source that writers and judges read. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#one-core-description-for-every-standard)
- Build each item on one official knowledge statement, and take its wrong options from the misconceptions recorded for that statement once they are confirmed not model-written. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#one-knowledge-statement-per-item)
- Keep each generated knowledge statement short and about one idea, since people trimmed the long ones and split the combined ones. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#one-short-idea-per-knowledge-statement)
- Pin the exact edition or translation of every source text, since its reading level and its rights can differ from one edition to the next. [record](groups/the-text.md#pin-the-edition-of-every-text)
- Pin the official exam specification a course targets by effective date and digest, and re-check every item type and generator schema against it whenever it changes. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#pin-the-exam-specification-by-date)
- Decide in code, before the model writes any prose, what each question covers and which fact its answer hinges on, and let the model only render that plan. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#plan-each-question-before-the-prose)
- Plan a sequence or group of related knowledge as a whole, with one target performance, a throughline and a persistent example, before splitting it. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#plan-the-whole-cluster-before-splitting-it)
- Treat a curriculum planning map as a guide for adults: never copy its rows into student material without separate review, or take a row as proof of learning. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#planning-maps-are-for-adults)
- Before checking a unit, confirm the grade, subject and reference files the check uses are that unit's own, since a check pointed at another unit's says nothing about this one. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#point-every-check-at-its-own-unit)
- Record where each field came from, not only each record, marking model-inferred content as unsourced with its reasoning, because one record can mix official text with generated fields. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#provenance-for-every-field)
- Before planning lessons or items, rank topics and stories by how much readers and models already know, key nothing to that knowledge, and test the best-known without the text. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#rank-each-topics-leak-risk-first)
- Check every word-part split, affix meaning and pronunciation against a dictionary or rule-based reference, never intuition, because invented splits teach false word-parts. [record](groups/the-text.md#real-word-parts-checked-against-a-reference)
- Take reference values such as test norms from a cited, versioned table, never from a model's memory. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#reference-values-come-from-a-cited-table)
- Expect knowledge drafted from several sources to overlap, and remove repeats across the whole course, not only within a unit, before people review it. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#remove-repeats-across-the-whole-course)
- Before reusing an inherited bank, generator or source pack, record what each is useful for and what it cannot establish, since a high item score can hide a false source. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#say-what-each-inherited-asset-cant-establish)
- Set each grade's voice, tone, praise, feedback length and pace on purpose for its learners' age, and never let a missing setting fall back to another grade's default. [record](groups/what-a-student-is-told.md#set-each-grades-voice-on-purpose)
- Size a course's length in measured minutes for each kind of activity, never by holding points constant, since equal points can take very different times. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#size-a-course-in-minutes-not-points)
- Before generating, size a unit's item bank from how many certified items each use needs, such as practice, checkpoints, retest forms and review, divided by the expected certification yield. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#size-generation-from-demand-and-yield)
- Size an adaptive bank between two limits: large enough that no item is over-exposed, small enough that each item can gather the responses its calibration needs. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#size-the-bank-between-two-limits)
- Size the time for an extended written answer from how long a top-scoring answer takes to write, and protect that time when a lesson runs long. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#size-writing-time-from-a-top-answer)
- Write what is to be known as small atoms of a few facts each, kept apart from the composite tasks that combine them. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#small-atoms-of-knowledge-then-composites)
- Back every real-world claim, and every non-fiction text, with a recorded, reputable source, and check each claim against that source, never against what sounds plausible. [record](groups/the-text.md#source-every-factual-claim)
- Specify a lesson by the observable functions it must perform, not by a fixed template, so any activity form that keeps each function visible can meet it. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#specify-lessons-by-function-not-template)
- Split a knowledge unit only when what counts as a right answer changes, and keep variants of difficulty, representation and method inside it. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#split-a-unit-only-when-success-changes)
- Standards bound what a course covers but not its order, so design the knowledge curriculum and its progression first and use standards to check coverage afterwards. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#standards-give-scope-not-sequence)
- For a text without a lesson question, draft several and keep one only on a judge's positive evidence that the text answers it. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#suggest-a-lesson-question-the-text-answers)
- Tag a text with a knowledge component only on a quoted span that shows it, not on trigger words. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#tag-a-text-only-on-quoted-evidence)
- Choose a word to teach only when a minimal pair can be written for it: the same situation with one feature changed, and a reason the non-example fails. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#teach-a-word-whose-minimal-pair-is-writable)
- When a choice rests on judged scores and weights, rerun it across the plausible weightings and with noise added to the scores, and report which choices hold and which flip. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#test-a-scored-choice-against-other-weights)
- When most learners carry no placement or mastery signal, design a routing plan's default branch as its main path and check it doesn't hold everyone back. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#the-default-branch-is-the-main-path)
- Write a lesson from its own design document and audit the built lesson part by part against it, because builds drop or adapt designed parts silently. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#the-design-document-is-the-build-spec)
- Choose how to teach by what the knowledge is like, rather than by the habits of its subject, since no method is good or bad in general. [record](groups/how-people-learn.md#the-kind-of-knowledge-sets-the-method)
- Let the platform, not the model, step a learner through each part of a problem in order, because a prompt alone didn't keep the model on track. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#the-platform-not-the-model-runs-the-steps)
- Before a writer or learner sees a source, verify that its link opens the claimed work and that the stored text is that whole work run to its end. [record](groups/the-text.md#the-stored-text-is-the-claimed-work)
- Type every prerequisite link as hard or soft and as logical, evidence-based or conventional, let only hard, non-conventional links block a learner, and invent no chains the subject lacks. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#type-and-justify-every-prerequisite)
- Keep missing, ambiguous and conflicting answers, sources and rules open until one dated, named decision settles them, never inferring, averaging or widening them, and label anything built on them provisional. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#unknown-stays-unknown-until-ruled-on)
- Make one component canonical and write the rest against it: list what later components may not re-ask, and let none of them contradict it. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#write-against-the-canonical-piece)
- Write down which source governs a lesson, its own most specific named design over generic rules or a sibling lesson, and sort new feedback against it before acting. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#write-down-which-source-governs)
- Have a model assemble, adapt and cite vetted sources, carrying over what they specify rather than inventing items from scratch. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#write-from-vetted-material-not-from-scratch)
- Write the course sequence before items: declare its policy and feed sources to a model in order of authority, never blended, then check the order it proposes against that policy. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#write-the-sequence-before-the-items)
- Check reference data before enforcing it, because a standard's record can carry a wrong rule, such as one fixed correct answer for every item, that would fail correct items. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#check-reference-data-before-enforcing-it)
- File each primary source under the period in which it was made, not the period it mentions, and check every period label against the source's date in code. [record](groups/the-text.md#file-a-source-by-when-it-was-made)
- Give a writer the exact objective, not just the topic: its official statement, its limits and exclusions, and the criteria and difficulty definitions its judge will check. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#give-the-writer-the-whole-objective)
- Give a writer verified facts and real sources, quoted into its instructions, as the only source it may draw on rather than as inspiration. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#verified-facts-as-the-only-source)

## 2. Write: draft the stimulus, question, options, guide and feedback

- Rebuild pages copied from a lesson on another procedure around the new procedure's steps, since new wording in old page shapes hides the change of task from learners. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#a-new-procedure-needs-its-own-pages)
- Let accuracy override any length or span quota in a writer's instructions, because a writer that can't meet a quota truthfully may bend the facts to meet it. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#accuracy-over-any-quota)
- Ask a choice question open only if its stem stands without the options; accept the right option in any words, and reply to a wrong one with its own feedback. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#ask-a-choice-question-open)
- Check that a question needs its passage by asking whether it would still work for a different passage on the same topic, and redesign it if it would. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#ask-whether-another-passage-would-do)
- For questions that share one source, choose the source first, list the distinct reasoning moves it supports, and write each question around a different move. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#build-a-set-from-its-source)
- Make a question need its text by how the text and the item format are built, not by filtering, patching or regenerating questions afterwards. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#build-questions-to-need-the-text)
- When questions test a text, build it from particulars that only it supplies and no student of the topic could already know, with every claim about the world still true. [record](groups/the-text.md#build-tested-texts-from-particulars)
- Tell a writer in concrete, checkable terms what the item must make the student do, not in abstract labels such as a taxonomy level or 'be specific'. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#concrete-checkable-instructions)
- Define a mastery task by worked answers and a rubric that scores the whole performance, rather than by a list of sub-skills. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#define-mastery-with-worked-answers-and-a-rubric)
- Expect to discard much of what a generator writes, keep the trace of what was accepted and rejected, and let the gates, not trust in the generator, carry the quality. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#dont-trust-the-generator)
- Give the writer the item's exact structure and make every field that matters required; without a structure items failed far more often, and fields left optional got left out. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#exact-structure-required-fields)
- Use a few vetted examples to show a writer an item's form, never its facts, since it copies their content and flaws: take them from other topics, stripped to structure. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#examples-teach-form-not-facts)
- Fix an item's structure and answer key first, write its explanations against that fixed key, then check every explanation, hint and feedback line against the actual options and key. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#fix-the-key-then-explain-it)
- Keep a clean voice-only master and separate music stems, so that a later edit to the picture or the score never forces regenerating approved speech. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#keep-a-voice-only-master-and-stems)
- Keep directions on how to read apart from the words to be read, and let them set only the tone: one voice model read its own instructions aloud. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#keep-directions-apart-from-the-words)
- Keep every line a student sees in the lesson's data, not the player: a line built into a player for one text can show up wrongly on other texts. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#keep-student-facing-lines-out-of-the-player)
- Keep the writer and the judge apart: void any item whose student-facing text addresses the judge, and show the writer plain editorial notes, never the judge's scores or raw output. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#keep-the-writer-and-the-judge-apart)
- Key each question to what only the text settles, never to an order, category, word sense or fact the world already supplies. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#key-it-to-what-only-the-text-holds)
- When a model splits material, have it mark each part's start and end and cut the original in code: asked to copy, it rewrites pictures, tables and diagrams as words. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#let-the-model-point-copy-in-code)
- Force a strict output format only on kinds of item where its cost has been measured, because it can cut accuracy sharply on reasoning-heavy content while helping classification-like tasks. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#measure-what-a-forced-format-costs)
- Don't hand a writer known misconceptions as loose suggestions for wrong options: it may write them into stems and explanations as if they were true. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#misconceptions-are-not-suggestions)
- Direct a narration voice so its mood never costs intelligibility: no exaggerated delivery that obscures the words, no flat list reading and no tone younger than the learners. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#mood-never-costs-clear-words)
- In an item that asks which source states a detail, use neutral labels and statements balanced across the sources' topics, so only reading the sources tells which one says what. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#neutral-labels-for-which-source-items)
- Never change a question's wording to feed an interface feature, such as adding a clause so an annotation has another word to mark. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#never-reword-a-question-for-the-interface)
- Keep words and labels out of generated images and video frames. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#no-text-in-generated-pictures)
- Ask a video generator for one main action per clip and no cut inside it, since a clip asked to carry a sequence produces one that cannot be edited out. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#one-action-per-generated-clip)
- Give each rule one home and authority, restated nowhere else, and check a writer's instructions for contradictions before generating, since conflicting rules cause refusals and silent overrides. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#one-home-for-each-rule)
- Make a complex item in steps that each do one job the writer can't do well alongside the others, rather than in a single pass. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#one-job-per-generation-step)
- Generate each item type through its own steps and its own checks, so that a change to one type cannot break another. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#one-pipeline-per-item-type)
- Differentiate by rewording one question over the same lines, knowledge and evidence, plainer for struggling readers and closer to the test for stronger ones, never by easier questions. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#one-question-reworded-for-each-stage)
- Give a writer only the rules that apply to the piece it is writing, compiled from the rulebook, never the whole rulebook. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#only-the-rules-that-apply)
- To have a learner commit before seeing options, ask for an answer in their own words first, keep it on screen, then have them check it against the choices. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#own-words-first-then-the-choices)
- When a model makes pictures or film, fix each recurring character, place and medium once per unit with a reused reference, since it has no memory between pictures. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#pin-what-a-picture-model-cant-keep)
- Plan each text in layers before writing it: what it states, what it implies with support, and plausible neighbouring ideas it does not support, kept for the wrong options. [record](groups/the-text.md#plan-the-text-in-layers)
- For items on an informational text, count one needing a predictive inference as far harder than one needing a bridging or pronoun-reference inference, which readers make reliably. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#predictive-inferences-are-much-harder)
- Have the voice read archaic wording for meaning in its natural voice, with no put-on period accent, and guide its pronunciation without respelling the printed quotation. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#read-archaic-wording-in-a-natural-voice)
- Let an automatic refine loop act only on structural failures and send a judge's quality findings to a person, since a refiner acting on them rewrote approved content. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#refine-on-structure-flag-quality)
- Refine a marking guide against real student answers before relying on it, rather than writing it in one pass. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#refine-the-marking-guide-on-real-answers)
- Judge a technical term by whether learners can build its concept now, and where only the word is too hard, give it a plain label and keep the idea. [record](groups/the-text.md#relabel-a-hard-word-keep-the-idea)
- Anything reused from another lesson or item, such as a check, step names, narration or a feedback line, must be rewritten for its new place. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#rewrite-whatever-is-reused)
- Voice generators given short lines can invent a continuation or speak their style notes, so mark where each clip's literal text starts and stops, and check short clips hardest. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#short-clips-invite-invented-continuations)
- Pass source texts and task material to a writer model as data apart from its instructions, so words inside a source cannot override the rules it was given. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#sources-go-in-as-data-not-instructions)
- Split narration into clips only at real sentence ends, never at an abbreviation's full stop, since a lone fragment like a title abbreviation can return far longer than its text. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#split-narration-only-at-sentence-ends)
- Meet a length limit by splitting the material into parts, not by cutting or speeding up approved words. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#split-dont-cut)
- Store a completed student or model response as its own evidence record, never inside the question, so it can't leak into the item and blind judging stays possible. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#store-a-response-apart-from-its-question)
- Tag every item with the standard it measures in its structured data, not in a title or the item text, using one code convention that resolves in the official framework. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#tag-each-items-standard-in-its-data)
- Time each on-screen reveal and caption from when the voice says its words, taken from an aligned take, not by spreading them evenly or by sentence length. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#time-each-reveal-from-aligned-speech)
- Warnings and bans don't reliably steer a writer: some invite the error they name, exact trap lists help without removing it, unchecked bans stay advisory; change what it's given instead. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#warnings-and-bans-dont-steer-a-writer)
- Where reviewed material exists, work from it rather than regenerating it from source notes, since regenerating throws away the review and brings back errors already caught. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#work-from-reviewed-material)
- Give every question a written answerability model showing how its key or each rubric point is earned from the delivered sources and declared knowledge, so another person can recompute it. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#write-each-items-answerability-model)
- Let a model write only the free text a lesson needs, and make every choice, mark and check a bounded judgment that, at play time, picks among lines written and checked in advance. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#write-little-judge-the-rest)
- Compose a feedback headline only after the verdict exists, from the same writer, because praise written before the verdict can congratulate a wrong answer above its own correction. [record](groups/what-a-student-is-told.md#write-the-headline-after-the-verdict)
- When generating story pictures, name for each scene who is in the frame and who is not, since one shared cast list put characters where the text has none. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#name-who-is-in-each-scene)
- When a check rejects a draft, regenerate only the stage that failed and pass the specific reason, not a generic 'try again'. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#regenerate-only-the-stage-that-failed)
- When a model generates the video, show a comparison as consecutive shots or a swap in place, not as two objects side by side in one frame. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#show-generated-comparisons-one-at-a-time)
- How can an open question ask about a contrast the text states outright without giving the answer away, when naming both sides nearly states it? [record](groups/not-settled.md#asking-about-a-contrast-the-text-states)

## 3. Settle: check in code everything code can decide

- Give a check an expected count from the content, code or a fixed list, never from what it finds, and fail by name on anything missing or unknown. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#a-check-counts-what-should-be-there)
- A check that never ran, ran on nothing or was out of service has not passed: count it, and anything made while it was down, as failed or unverified. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#a-check-not-run-has-not-passed)
- Flag an answer as copied only when it recites a long unbroken run of the source, not for shared words, which correct answers about a short text will reuse. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#a-copy-guard-catches-recitation-not-overlap)
- When a new rule meets older content, list each existing breach as debt that warns every run, and fail anything unlisted and any stale entry, so the list only shrinks. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#a-debt-list-that-can-only-shrink)
- Check in code that every keyed quotation occurs exactly once in its passage, since a quotation that recurs elsewhere leaves the evidence ambiguous. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#a-keyed-quote-occurs-once-in-its-passage)
- Check every generated voice clip against its script and measure how loud its first word is, because a matching transcript doesn't show the clip can be heard. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#a-matching-transcript-isnt-an-audible-clip)
- Let a computed check overrule a judge only on the property it measured and only beyond its own error margin. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#a-measure-overrules-only-what-it-measured)
- Every rule that fails safe by going quiet, such as missing audio playing as silence, needs its own detector, or its failures stay invisible to other checks. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#a-quiet-failure-needs-its-own-detector)
- Record each content rule's scope and what checks it, because a rule nothing checks is only a reminder. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#a-rule-nothing-checks-is-a-reminder)
- A check triggered by a word list fires on ordinary uses of its words, so make it require an explicit phrase and filter out narrative and negated uses. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#a-trigger-word-is-not-a-directive)
- A wrong option absent from the text is usually a sound contrast, so never report absence from the text as a defect on its own. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#absent-from-the-text-is-not-a-fault)
- Telling an agent not to change content doesn't stop it: hash content files before and after automated work, and keep every change as an edit that can be replayed. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#an-instruction-is-not-a-guard)
- After items are built or rebuilt, submit the right answer, each wrong answer and an incomplete one to the live scorer, since scoring that loaded can still be wrong. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#answer-every-item-in-the-live-scorer)
- Bind each marked word in a quotation to its exact position, and refuse a mark named only by a word that recurs there rather than guess which one. [record](groups/the-text.md#bind-each-mark-to-its-place)
- Bind each question to the exact text it was written and checked against, and confirm that pairing on the delivered form as a blocking check of its own. [record](groups/the-text.md#bind-each-question-to-its-text)
- Build what a student sees from an allowlist of the fields meant for students, and test that view for answer leaks. [record](groups/the-text.md#build-the-student-view-from-an-allowlist)
- Report a measured value that falls within the tolerance margin of a limit as borderline, not as in range. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#call-a-borderline-value-borderline)
- Make captions in code from the approved script and the clip's measured length, never by hand, and fail any video that gates progress if it has no captions. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#captions-from-the-approved-script)
- Check that a model marker returns exactly one verdict per authored criterion, and treat a missing, invented, duplicated or malformed verdict as a marking failure, never a pass or miss. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#check-a-markers-reply-against-its-criteria)
- Never take a speech transcript as proof the learner spoke: check it against how much voice the recording held, since a recogniser can invent answers from silence. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#check-a-transcript-against-the-voice-heard)
- Check every line a student reads, passages, options, keys, definitions and every rewrite, for the subject's known misconceptions, flagging hits in code before publication whoever wrote it. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#check-every-part-and-every-version)
- Where a task promises all of something or a range, check that every member is there. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#check-every-promise-a-task-makes)
- Check each grade and standard tag outside the generator, against the delivered text and the skill it tests. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#check-every-tag-outside-the-generator)
- Check hints and wrong-answer replies for leaks in code: none may carry a word only the key or its criterion's answers carry, not counting the criterion's framing words. [record](groups/what-a-student-is-told.md#check-leaks-by-words-only-the-key-carries)
- Check every line a model writes at answer time against house rules and the authored text beside it, repairing rather than discarding what fails, such as praise repeating the answer. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#check-lines-written-at-answer-time)
- Check what an output must contain as well as what it must avoid, so an empty or minimal output that breaks no rule still fails. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#check-obligations-as-well-as-prohibitions)
- When a rule spaces a film's questions apart, check which placements it leaves: a rule that bars neighbouring parts can leave a short film only one. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#check-where-spacing-rules-put-questions)
- Let code accept an answer without the judge only if that answer was checked on its own; answers checked only as a list go to the judge one by one. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#code-accepts-only-answers-checked-one-by-one)
- Hard-block in code only what code detects reliably, keep keyword and length counts advisory, and check quality and correctness separately, since a well-formed item can be weak or wrong. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#code-gates-catch-broken-items-not-weak-ones)
- Deduplicate and count items on their full content, not on an opening prefix or an earlier step's count. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#deduplicate-on-the-full-content)
- Keep every count, figure, rule and setting in one named, dated, re-runnable source, and derive every document and copy from it rather than editing each by hand. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#every-count-from-one-dated-pull)
- Make sure every field a student sees or hears is read by at least one check. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#every-field-a-student-meets-is-checked)
- Check that every sentence mark in a read-along narration clip sits in a pause, because a mark inside speech cuts off the previous line's last word. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#every-sentence-mark-sits-in-a-pause)
- Check that every misconception a lesson means to catch is keyed by a wrong option or judged answer and routed to its own feedback, or it goes undetected. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#every-targeted-misconception-can-surface)
- Have a check fail on any exemption or known-fault entry that nothing current matches. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#exemptions-leave-with-what-they-exempt)
- Find repeated questions with a code check for near-identical wording, which gives the same answer every run, and list a judge's same-meaning pairs separately, at lower confidence. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#find-repeats-in-code-then-by-meaning)
- Work out each item's guessing floor from the scoring rule actually used where it is served, not from its format label. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#find-the-guessing-floor-as-scored)
- When a lesson's passage and its fixed source text disagree, change the lesson, never the source. [record](groups/the-text.md#fix-the-lesson-never-the-source)
- Flag any narration clip longer than its text predicts, and look for added words before blaming slow delivery, since extra length usually means the voice spoke beyond the script. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#flag-a-clip-longer-than-its-words)
- Run blocking content checks on every path that writes content, hand edits included, and roll back any write that fails them. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#guard-every-path-that-writes-content)
- Every rule that can refuse an answer must accept one that does exactly what the question asks, so test it on the wanted answer in its expected words. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#guards-must-accept-what-the-question-asks)
- A check that matches answers to the text must keep every sentence that carries a content word, however short. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#keep-every-sentence-however-short)
- Keep a worked example and the independent check of the same idea on different screens, and fail any option sharing five or more consecutive words with on-screen teaching text. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#keep-teaching-and-its-check-apart)
- Check in code that every keyed span and candidate in a select-in-the-text task is exactly a unit the player lets students select, or the right answer can't be chosen. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#key-only-units-the-player-can-select)
- Take the text a check reads from an explicit list of the fields students see, not by excluding the rest. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#list-the-fields-a-check-reads)
- Check every named fact in generated content against the unit, period and standard it must stay within with a lookup, since prompt warnings reduce out-of-scope facts but don't remove them. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#look-up-every-named-facts-scope)
- Mark every model answer or reveal with the rubric it models before it ships. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#mark-each-model-answer-with-its-rubric)
- Measure each narration clip's loudness and match the clips across a lesson, since gain alone under a true-peak ceiling may not bring them close enough. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#match-loudness-across-narration-clips)
- Take every length and time from the delivered file, leave a value empty until it is measured, label an estimate as one, and stop the build rather than guess. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#measure-from-the-delivered-file)
- Check that the content itself is in view, the first real passage paragraph and the settled feedback with its next action, by measuring those nodes, never a wrapper. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#measure-the-content-not-its-container)
- When code scores a written answer by word overlap with a key, keep negation words such as 'not' as content words, since dropping them can reverse an answer's meaning. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#negation-counts-in-word-overlap-scoring)
- An exemption from a check is granted by someone other than whoever claims it, never self-declared, and must put a measured outcome in place of each one it waives. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#no-exemption-without-a-cost)
- Test a lesson in one complete learner run from an empty state through its finish, reloading partway and after saving, and check that progress, gates, rewards and saved work survive. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#one-complete-run-through-finish-and-reload)
- Treat a teaching piece that runs past the length ceiling for its kind of idea as a sign that it merges two ideas or is padded. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#past-the-length-ceiling-means-two-ideas)
- Let model-written feedback name a place in the text, such as its first line, only when the model was given each excerpt's position; otherwise block such claims in code. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#point-to-a-place-only-when-known)
- Prove narration plays by observing real playback where media can play, since simulated media events test only fallback code. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#prove-narration-plays-by-real-playback)
- When feedback quotes the learner, check in code that every quoted phrase is the learner's own words in order or the text's, and never shorten or belittle what they wrote. [record](groups/what-a-student-is-told.md#quote-the-learner-word-for-word)
- Read the checker's own code to learn exactly what it grades and parses, and match each item type's output structure to it, checked in code before producing at scale. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#read-the-checkers-code-first)
- Strip markup, tables and equations before estimating reading level, and check mechanical faults such as merged words or stray symbols separately, because a readability estimate measures only complexity. [record](groups/the-text.md#reading-level-measures-complexity-only)
- Never trust a pass label or measured value that arrives with content, such as a certification, a length, a reading level or a verbatim flag; recompute it on arrival. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#recompute-what-arrives-with-content)
- Record where every asset came from and its hash, check the hash before using it, and treat a source note as a record of origin, not a licence to reuse. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#record-where-every-asset-came-from)
- Before checking a file, confirm it is whole, such as ending where it must and holding its anchors, and refuse otherwise, because a truncated copy can pass every assertion. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#refuse-to-check-a-half-written-file)
- Render a course's extremes, its longest word, option and sentence and its largest set of options, at phone, tablet and desktop widths, and again whenever new content extends one. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#render-the-extremes-at-every-width)
- Repair in code what needs no judgment, such as quotation marks missing round the text's exact words, and send back to the writer only what does. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#repair-mechanical-slips-in-code)
- Record every defect a check finds, even when the item has already failed for another reason, and keep evaluating, so everything can be fixed in one round. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#report-every-defect-in-one-pass)
- Run every check on every real item before a generated set feeds anything that matters, not on a sample or only on the fixtures it was proven on. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#run-a-proven-check-on-every-real-item)
- Run each check only on the kinds of item it was written for. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#run-each-check-only-where-it-fits)
- Make every verdict reproducible: judge at the most deterministic setting and freeze or cache each machine judgment, so the same input always gets the same verdict. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#same-answer-same-verdict)
- Scan every line a student reads for internal contradictions: mismatched counts, one term used two ways, directions that don't fit the interaction, and references to things not on the page. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#scan-student-text-for-contradictions)
- Score an answer that has a key in code and pick its feedback by lookup from the score and option chosen, so no model decides either. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#score-keyed-answers-in-code)
- Settle in code everything code can decide, such as format, parts and key validity, before a judge sees the item, then give the judge those results as binding facts. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#settle-in-code-then-bind-the-judge)
- Make the skill list a check uses fine enough to separate the skills a lesson might confuse, because a switch between skills sharing one label passes every label-based check. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#skill-labels-fine-enough-to-tell-apart)
- Trace every threshold a check enforces to its source before failing content on it, because a gate without a sourced reason is a preference. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#source-every-threshold-a-check-enforces)
- Count spelling only where the word itself was taught: a taught word recalled from memory must be spelled exactly; elsewhere a slip is named but not marked wrong. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#spelling-counts-where-the-word-is-taught)
- Check that a read-along voice says only the words its page prints, exactly as printed, by tying each clip to its exact text and comparing whole transcribed lines. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#spoken-words-match-printed-words)
- Sweep everything a student is served, as it renders, for authoring leftovers such as working notes, markup tokens, escaped characters and placeholder codes, which instructions alone don't prevent. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#sweep-what-students-see-for-authoring-leftovers)
- Test a lesson's gates with its real components or with stand-ins proven to behave the same, because a stand-in that grants completion can hide a page that stays locked. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#test-gates-with-the-real-components)
- Where a person reviews the output anyway, let a check fire only on positive evidence that the fault is there and abstain when it can't tell. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#tune-checks-toward-silence)
- Turn every note in the content that warns of a fragile coupling, such as "must match" or "silently", into an automatic check, since each marks a silent failure. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#turn-fragile-coupling-notes-into-checks)
- Decide in code whether words are forms of the same word by looking them up in a published table of word forms, never by spelling rules. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#word-forms-from-a-table-not-rules)
- Gate a reading-level band only on texts you wrote; on published texts you chose, measure and report it, since they are not yours to rewrite to a number. [record](groups/the-text.md#gate-reading-level-only-on-your-texts)
- When no valid item can be made, fail visibly or serve a vetted item, never a weak, fallback or filler one, and refuse item types whose output was never measured. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#refuse-rather-than-ship-a-weak-item)
- Size output length caps from the longest real outputs plus headroom and check every delivered item is complete, since a cap or timeout can silently cut it short. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#size-output-caps-from-real-outputs)
- Write any check that depends on where things sit against a shared template, never against one example's arrangement, or it won't carry to the next one. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#write-layout-checks-against-the-template)

## 4. Judge: ask bounded questions of an independent judge

- Use a judge to check a bounded claim, not to hunt for faults. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#a-judge-checks-it-doesnt-hunt)
- Where the key is a judgment call, such as the stronger of two answers, ship only when a judge that never saw the key picks it by a clear margin. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#a-judgment-call-key-needs-a-blind-pick)
- Scan a judge's reasoning in code for a fault it admits and then excuses on grounds outside the rubric, and overturn or re-check that pass. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#a-pass-whose-reasoning-admits-a-fault)
- Beside every judgment of a student's words, ask whether they are aimed at the marker rather than the task. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#aimed-at-the-marker)
- When scoring whether an item is personalised to a student's interests, count an interest only when the student-facing text names it, and keep that score out of every correctness score. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#an-interest-counts-only-when-named)
- Accept a right answer nobody planned for, but only when the judge is sure, and log it for review. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#answers-nobody-planned-for)
- Back each check with one that pulls the other way, so that no single relaxed default lets an item pass. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#back-each-check-with-an-opposing-one)
- Let a grader pick only among drafts passing the code checks, after showing on saved candidates that it tracks the real criterion and beats taking the first valid one. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#best-of-n-inherits-its-grader)
- Certify a generated item only if it passes every identical re-grading, treating a flip as a sign of a real defect. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#certify-only-what-passes-every-re-grade)
- Nothing that wrote, picked or was optimised against an item certifies it: confirm with an uninvolved check from another model family on fresh cases, and read unshared gains as gaming. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#certify-with-an-independent-judge)
- Check wrong-option feedback for the right answer said in other words, which a word-match check misses and a truth check never asks about. [record](groups/what-a-student-is-told.md#check-feedback-for-the-answer-reworded)
- Check first that an item tests exactly the topic or skill, the difficulty and the format that were asked for, and let any miss fail it. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#check-fit-to-the-request-first)
- Check a media script before the media is made, because fixing content after recording means a costly re-record. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#check-the-script-before-the-film)
- Before one judge sample's critical failure voids a whole lesson, draw more samples and let the veto stand only when most of them fail. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#confirm-a-veto-before-it-voids)
- Judge whether two answers are equivalent under the convention being taught, since answers that are equal in general can differ in meaning under the lesson's convention. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#equivalent-under-the-convention-taught)
- Fail an item on picture evidence only when that evidence was measured or the error is gross, because a model's free description of an image can misplace fine detail. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#fail-on-a-picture-only-when-measured)
- Before an evidence item is used, ask the judge whether each other relevant line also proves the claim, and add every line it is sure of to the key. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#find-every-line-that-proves-the-claim)
- Give a judge exactly what the student sees, one bounded question at a time. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#give-the-judge-what-the-student-sees)
- A marker can apply a rubric's rule only if it is given what the rule refers to; otherwise enforce the rule in code. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#give-the-marker-what-the-rule-names)
- When one prompt asks a model to judge several items, treat identical verdicts on different items as a reason to re-check them. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#identical-verdicts-on-different-items-are-suspect)
- Check an interpretation by whether the text contradicts it and whether a careful reader could defend it from the passage, keeping truth checks for plain claims. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#judge-a-reading-as-defensible-not-true)
- Judge an item's difficulty against validated items for its grade, weighing the inference it demands and the strength of its wrong options, not by a model's direct rating. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#judge-difficulty-for-the-target-learners)
- Judge each kind of item only with checks and a rubric written for its type and format, run only on content positively identified as that kind. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#judge-each-type-by-its-own-criteria)
- Tell a judge to decide only from the words given, such as a question's own wording for give-aways, never from what it or readers know of a well-known work. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#judge-only-from-the-words-given)
- When a task asks for an original example, give the judge the taught meaning as its reference, never a closed list of acceptable answers, which contradicts the task. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#judge-original-examples-against-the-meaning)
- Check every generated item independently, since a writer's score on teaching questions, its grasp of a pedagogy and its own report of compliance are no evidence of what it makes. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#judge-the-artifact-not-the-writer)
- Judge only what changes the student's experience, and never fail an item for how its data is stored. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#judge-the-experience-not-the-storage)
- Judge an item by what it actually asks students to do, not by its command verb or what its notes and explanation claim. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#judge-what-the-task-demands)
- Model judges favour longer answers and one position, so length-normalise a marker's inputs, swap the order of compared answers, and test it on short full-credit answers. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#keep-a-marker-from-rewarding-length)
- Keep every judgment whole, so it can be replayed or re-asked of another judge; where two judges agree against an answer key, check the key. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#keep-every-judgment-whole)
- Keep what students write and show it back; a model's confident reading can choose a hint or move on, but not give a grade. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#keep-the-words-a-hint-not-a-grade)
- Give each known fault a narrow check that fails by default, since a judge that passes whatever is arguable talks itself past known faults. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#known-faults-fail-by-default)
- Keep a rubric's format rules apart from its subject, curriculum and grade specifics, and supply the standard's text at evaluation time rather than writing it into the rubric. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#layer-a-rubric-by-concern)
- Independent fact checkers can catch different errors, so let either one fail an item rather than trust a single checker, and expect more run-to-run variance in return. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#let-either-fact-checker-fail-it)
- When a model answers an item with the text hidden, count its repeated exact recoveries of the key as leaks, even when it says it is unsure or guessing. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#low-confidence-doesnt-erase-a-blind-hit)
- Mark meaning, not wording: any wording that says it counts, a short answer can be enough, and a point made on an earlier try still counts. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#mark-meaning-not-wording)
- No check passes by default when what it inspects or needs is missing, because otherwise leaving a part out becomes the easiest way to pass. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#no-pass-by-default)
- Call an answer off task only on the judge's confident no, and never when it meets any criterion. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#off-task-only-on-a-sure-no)
- Map each kind of defect to a single criterion and score in one place, so a fault can't fail several criteria or land on different scores across runs. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#one-defect-one-score)
- In a set of questions judged together, one factual error can fail the whole set, so check each question on its own before the set is judged. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#one-error-fails-the-whole-set)
- With many candidates, ask one yes-or-no question about each and combine the answers in code. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#one-question-per-candidate)
- Where a model's absolute marks drift, have it place each answer against human-scored anchor answers bracketing each score boundary, since models judge relative quality more stably than absolute scores. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#place-answers-against-scored-anchors)
- To anticipate where learners will struggle, have a model predict their difficulties from learner data rather than role-play a struggling student. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#predict-struggles-never-simulate-students)
- Make a judge quote the text each criterion applies to before it scores, and have it score only from those quotes. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#quote-the-evidence-before-the-verdict)
- Spend repeat judgments where a verdict sits near its threshold: judge a borderline item again, and break a disagreement with a third run. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#re-judge-the-borderline-cases)
- Speech recognisers can drop a short clip's last word, so before regenerating a clip flagged for a missing word, recheck it with review-only silence added and a stronger, calibrated recogniser. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#recheck-a-dropped-final-word-first)
- When a judged question fails most items because of a deliberate design choice, reword it before it gates anything. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#reword-a-question-that-fails-the-design)
- Check that a question needs its new text the hard way: give the solver everything else the student has met, and judge by the worst of several option orders. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#run-the-without-text-check-the-hard-way)
- Scan generated student-facing text for fused words such as 'themain' or 'tothe', and fail it when one appears, because early readers may not recognise them as words. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#scan-for-fused-words)
- Check that a text generator is set to the learners' level before each batch, and screen every text for difficulty, cohesion, age-appropriateness and sensitive content before students see it. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#screen-generated-texts-for-the-learners-age)
- A check certifies only the property it tests, so give structure, facts, design, text dependence and teaching quality each a blocking check of its own. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#separate-checks-for-separate-properties)
- Keep a question only when solvers outside the generator's model family all fail it blind and answer it with the text, failure there often meaning ambiguity or a broad key. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#solve-it-blind-and-with-the-text)
- Put a student's words in a field of their own in the judge's input and ask about that field by name, never inside the question's wording. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#student-words-in-their-own-field)
- Besides checking that a quote is exact, check that the passage itself states everything the claim says, not merely that the claim is plausible, and audit 'partly supported' verdicts. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#supported-means-the-passage-says-it)
- Don't mark a word as too hard when the material teaches it, and fail reading level only on several untaught words or constructions that are off-level. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#taught-words-are-never-too-hard)
- Test a question's fit to the curriculum by checking that the unit's official scope can answer it. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#test-fit-against-the-official-scope)
- A listening model can report sounds the narration only describes, so test a reported sound effect on the same audio reversed, and leave remaining disagreement to a person. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#test-reported-sound-effects-on-reversed-audio)
- Judge stand-alone instruction by whether a newcomer could answer most of its check questions after one pass; if they could not, the instruction failed, not the learner. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#the-teaching-failed-not-the-learner)
- Trace every statement in generated text to a given source fact, and drop or flag any that traces to none, because a model's memory never counts as support. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#trace-every-statement-to-a-source)
- Transcribe a film's narration from its audio track alone, because a model watching the video can mix on-screen labels into what it reports hearing and drop spoken words. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#transcribe-film-narration-from-audio-alone)
- Write each judged check as a typed question with its passing answer fixed in advance, and let code, not the judge, turn the answer into a verdict. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#typed-checks-with-a-fixed-passing-answer)
- An unsure judge has said neither yes nor no: unsure is never a pass and never a miss, the student keeps the answer, and a part the judge can't confirm is left out. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#unsure-is-neither-yes-nor-no)
- Void any item or evaluation whose content addresses the judge, such as pre-written scores, metric-by-metric self-praise, claims to be a worked example, fake reveal cues or instructions to pass. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#void-items-that-address-the-judge)
- Enforce a standard strictly only when the request names it, treat an inferred one as guidance, and use none rather than a near match when the named one is missing. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#enforce-a-standard-only-as-far-as-known)
- Let a fact check fail an item only when it can state the wrong claim and a well-documented correction. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#fail-a-fact-only-with-its-correction)
- Let a written-answer marker return graded, revise or human review, sending ambiguity, conflicting evidence, rubric gaps and low confidence to a person instead of forcing a verdict. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#graded-revise-or-human-review)
- Count an answer found without the text as a leak only when a typical student at the target level could find it the same way, not merely a strong model. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#judge-leaks-at-the-students-level)

## 5. Review: people and scripted readers check what the judge can't

- When a text or specification fails a check, carry the failure to every lesson, item, chart and piece of feedback built on it. [record](groups/the-text.md#a-failed-source-fails-what-depends-on-it)
- A quota balances only the level it counts, so set and check targets for the standards and formats inside each domain, not only for the domains. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#a-quota-balances-only-what-it-counts)
- Let a reference course inform only interaction and presentation: it never proves a verdict, content correctness or grade fit, and none of its content, numbers or policies carry across. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#a-reference-course-shows-presentation-only)
- A tapped sentence in a read-along must play only itself, so test non-adjacent taps on every passage, since listening straight through cannot reveal a clip that runs on. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#a-tapped-sentence-plays-only-itself)
- A wrong fact in the text, the key or any line a student reads is never a polish note: it holds the item back until fixed. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#a-wrong-fact-is-never-polish)
- In a review panel, an evidence-backed objection stands until whoever raised it withdraws it after a traced repair, and no vote settles a question of fact. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#an-objection-stands-until-withdrawn)
- Choose a narration voice from auditioned, timed takes of the real script, judged by what is heard and checked word for word, not by catalogue descriptions or requested settings. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#audition-voices-before-making-every-clip)
- Judge content by what the delivered player shows, checking files, the student's live experience and course claims apart, and count an authored element or gate only once seen working. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#authored-is-not-delivered)
- Tie every check's result to the digest of the build and screen it checked, so a relevant change withdraws the result and reruns the affected checks. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#bind-each-check-to-what-it-checked)
- Score compared versions blind to which system or maker produced each, in shuffled order, and have a second person independently score a preset share plus every disputed or severe case. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#blind-shuffled-and-double-scored-review)
- Check that every item marked must-learn comes back for retrieval from memory in a later lesson, since a unit plan can leave whole lessons' must-learn items never recalled. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#bring-back-every-must-learn-item)
- Check what a machine made against something that doesn't assume the answer. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#check-against-something-that-doesnt-assume-the-answer)
- Check every claim a text presents as true about the world at the precision its grade needs. [record](groups/the-text.md#check-every-world-claim)
- Review generated text and pictures for stereotyped or insensitive portrayals of people. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#check-how-people-are-portrayed)
- Check every line of narration against the lesson's key idea, because a single line can contradict the thesis the lesson is teaching. [record](groups/what-a-student-is-told.md#check-narration-against-the-key-idea)
- When review rounds settle into cosmetic findings, check the design against the target it claims to serve, because quiet rounds can hide a real gap. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#check-the-design-against-its-target)
- Spot-check a generator's hardest requests most closely, since pass rates can fall steeply as the requested difficulty rises and requests for upper grades can come back simpler than asked. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#check-the-hardest-requests-most)
- When narration paraphrases or embeds a quoted line, check that its negatives still say what the line says, since a carried-over negative can make a double negative that reverses it. [record](groups/what-a-student-is-told.md#check-the-negatives-around-a-quoted-line)
- Check a sequence of texts or lessons as a whole: a fact must agree from one to the next, and true steps must not misteach in the order given. [record](groups/the-text.md#check-the-sequence-not-only-the-parts)
- Check every generated or repainted picture by eye against its brief and the text, and draw maps and diagrams from data. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#check-what-a-picture-implies)
- Check, item by item rather than from tags or averages, that each item and lesson actually demands the skill, standard, topic and passage its labels claim. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#check-what-an-item-demands-not-its-tag)
- When content or code is shared or ported between courses or levels, compare their conventions one by one and check what the shared code assumes. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#check-what-shared-code-assumes)
- Give every block a short label that is never renumbered or reused, name every automatic finding by it, and show the labels beside any page that carries none. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#cite-each-block-by-a-fixed-label)
- Treat a finding as settled only when independent methods or sources reach it. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#count-only-independent-agreement)
- Before a review calls a lesson's support missing, count the choices, labels, captions and images on screen, and judge only what is needed at that moment. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#count-visible-support-before-calling-it-missing)
- Name what works in a lesson, specifically, as well as what fails, because an audit that concedes nothing reads as rhetoric. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#credit-what-works-specifically)
- Accept a claim only on evidence of its kind, runtime evidence for behaviour, independent review of the exact text for meaning, access testing for access, however much weaker evidence agrees. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#evidence-of-the-kind-the-claim-makes)
- Every example in a standard's description must obey the rules beside it; when a rule and its example disagree, decide which is the source of truth and fix the other. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#examples-must-obey-their-rules)
- Score generated material for rule-following and for quality in separate blind passes, and keep the generation rules out of the quality pass. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#hide-the-rules-from-the-quality-pass)
- Give a reviewing or play-testing model a staged copy of its allowed inputs and nothing else, since an instruction or a read-only sandbox doesn't stop it reading more. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#isolate-a-model-by-staging-its-inputs)
- Judge each narration clip at the speed learners will hear it, since a voice that sounds fine at natural pace can be unintelligible when sped up. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#judge-narration-at-ship-speed)
- Check a bank, form or course as a set as well as item by item: find repeats, near-duplicates and items that give away, prime or contradict one another. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#judge-the-set-as-well-as-each-item)
- Check each screen as a whole at each moment, and withdraw used scaffolding once the learner succeeds, because correct parts can add up to clutter or two asks at once. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#judge-the-whole-screen-as-it-changes)
- Run each simulated review perspective in its own process, blind to the others' prompts and results until code combines them. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#keep-simulated-review-perspectives-apart)
- Generate from the content itself a list of every learner-facing line that is not word for word from the approved source, so each can be approved, rewritten or cut. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#list-every-line-not-in-the-source)
- Log every deliberate divergence from the design with the ruling that covers it, and say where the live build differs from what was authored instead of reconciling it quietly. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#log-every-divergence-from-the-design)
- Record every review objection that was overruled, with its reasoning, and check it again in the next round, so a recurrence counts as evidence rather than memory. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#log-overruled-objections-and-recheck-them)
- Keep machine checks apart from human judgment: a quality call stays unjudged until a person has watched or listened, and an AI judge only picks what that person reviews. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#machine-checks-are-not-judgment)
- Make the changes and run the quicker checks before one full walk-through of a lesson, rather than walking, failing, patching and walking again. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#make-the-changes-then-walk-once)
- Keep music under the narration, lowest where it teaches, and check the final mix by ear for clear words, not only the clean voice track. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#music-stays-under-the-words)
- Let a recorded human override settle a judgement call, but never let it clear a factual, safety or integrity failure. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#no-override-clears-a-factual-failure)
- Build and fully review one complete, representative slice of lessons, by hand if need be, before automating or making the rest, and make it the template. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#one-clean-slice-before-the-course)
- Treat a single manual review as a floor: a second read, blind to the first, finds what the first missed. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#one-manual-read-is-a-floor)
- Proofread every line a student reads or hears, model answers first, since validators miss grammar slips and the repeated stock openers of machine writing. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#proofread-every-line-students-meet)
- Prove coverage by counting how many lessons and items serve each required skill, reading each scored item against the standard's boundary, and treat a zero as a gap. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#prove-coverage-item-by-item)
- Qualify anyone whose judgments will set a standard on a fresh blind test, because familiarity is not qualification. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#qualify-judges-blind)
- Qualify anyone whose labels will set a standard on boundary cases and their opposite-value twins, not only clear cases, since clear-case accuracy can hide shared boundary failures. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#qualify-on-boundary-cases-and-twins)
- Rank findings by effect on learners: anything that can mislead, falsely reject, block or wrongly advance a learner holds the work, polish comes last, and preferences aren't defects. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#rank-findings-by-their-effect-on-learners)
- Check a simplified text by reading it against its original, because a better reading-level score can hide a changed meaning, lost key terms and mechanical prose. [record](groups/the-text.md#read-a-simplified-text-against-its-original)
- Test a worked example by reading it with the source sentences hidden: if what remains reads as a list of correct answers, it models answers, not thinking, and fails. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#read-a-worked-example-without-its-text)
- Read the failing items and the judge's own reasoning, not just the scores and flag fields, because a summary can hide what failed and why. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#read-the-failing-items-themselves)
- When long words, such as a taught word, lift a readability score, judge whether an item reads too hard by its sentence length and clause depth instead. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#readability-by-sentence-not-by-formula)
- Re-verify every dated claim and source on recent events against a current source before release: a curriculum bundle is no authority on what happened after its build date. [record](groups/the-text.md#recent-claims-need-a-current-source)
- Treat identical generic comments across reviewed pages as false coverage: mark those pages unobserved and review each again, asking what the learner has read or heard and must do. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#repeated-generic-comments-mean-unobserved-pages)
- Review a lesson in the mode students get: one player's reviewer mode let a seek skip its checks, and the students' mode never did. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#review-as-a-student)
- Review in rounds, each through a new lens and again after every change, fixes and display code included, and stop only after two rounds in a row find nothing new. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#review-in-rounds-each-with-a-new-lens)
- Before content goes to an outside review, run your own review standard over the exact build being sent, as an adversarial pass split by dimension, and fix what it finds. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#run-your-own-review-standard-first)
- Make every check and audit list, item by item, what it read and skipped, and treat the rest as unchecked until covered: a clean result proves only what was read. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#say-what-an-audit-didnt-check)
- Never generate a course from a single approved lesson: make and review lessons in batches of about three, each showing varied skill teaching, building knowledge and some fading of support. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#scale-a-course-in-reviewed-batches)
- Before a person sees a lesson, have scripted readers play it: one who knows, one who guesses, one who is stuck, and one who tries to game it. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#scripted-readers-first)
- Check a claimed knowledge sequence by working through the delivered lessons: the pre-teaching, the link and the deeper revisit must be visible, or the claim doesn't count. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#show-the-sequence-dont-claim-it)
- Test an answer gate between selecting and submitting, including a right answer not yet submitted and one then changed, since a correct end screen cannot show an early release. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#test-a-gate-between-select-and-submit)
- Before release, run every authored wrong-answer check and every meaningful return to a phase, and treat generated combinations of test conditions as diagnostic inventory, not a requirement. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#test-every-authored-path-not-every-combination)
- Test every control in a film or scene player for an unintended way past unseen content, since the main flow can pass its tests while a side control skips it. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#test-every-player-control-for-skips)
- Test feedback on learners who just answered wrong: it works if they can say what went wrong and where to reread, and its mere presence proves nothing. [record](groups/what-a-student-is-told.md#test-feedback-on-the-learner-who-missed)
- Test whatever marks an answer when the model is slow or down, because that path marks real students too. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#test-the-fallback)
- Count a line in a film before a reading as setting only if it needs no 'then', no character's choice made it true, and it holds without reading the passage. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#three-tests-for-setting-or-plot)
- A question frame designed on one kind of text must be tried on each other kind before it is used there. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#try-a-frame-on-each-kind-of-text)
- Before filing or fixing a finding, confirm it on the current, settled, live build, test the check that raised it on clean content, and publish how many were withdrawn. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#verify-a-finding-before-fixing-it)
- Write each finding as one falsifiable problem with its exact location, the offending text word for word, why it matters, a concrete fix and the rechecks that show it fixed. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#write-findings-that-can-be-checked)

## 6. Measure: measure quality and learning the same way each time

- A check that has never fired proves nothing until it catches a fault planted for it. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#a-check-must-catch-a-planted-fault)
- Before trusting a check's rejections, run it over content known to be good, and treat its flags there as faults in the check. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#a-check-must-pass-a-known-good-case)
- Count a fault as caught only when its own check flags the right place, never on a crash or failing run, and fail runs printing failures, whatever their exit code. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#a-checks-signal-must-match-its-finding)
- Treat any difficulty, threshold or cut score set before learners have answered as a labelled prior, and replace it with live response data. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#a-difficulty-set-before-use-is-a-prior)
- A fatal fault, such as a wrong key, a wrong fact or off-topic content, fails the item, so each needs a blocking check rather than a share of an average. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#a-fatal-fault-cant-be-averaged-away)
- Calibrate a leak check's blind solver, which answers without the text, on items known to leak and items known to be clean before trusting its leak rate. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#a-leak-check-needs-a-calibrated-solver)
- Check that a rubric's rules can all be met at once. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#a-rubrics-rules-must-fit-together)
- Give each question a target success rate and treat a result far from it, either way, as a fault in the question. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#a-target-success-rate-per-question)
- A verdict holds only for the pinned model version that gave it, so compare runs under one version and, when it changes, re-score everything, baseline and blind solves included. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#a-verdict-belongs-to-its-model)
- Treat acquisition, durable mastery and transfer as three claims, each needing its own evidence, and never let a check built for one stand in for another. [record](groups/how-people-learn.md#acquisition-durability-and-transfer-are-separate-claims)
- Agreement between a judge's samples is consistency, not correctness, and its choices can shift with the order of the options. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#agreement-is-not-correctness)
- Audit a judge with matched inputs that differ only in something that should not matter, such as an incidental item feature or the prompt's formatting, before trusting its scores. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#audit-a-judge-with-matched-pairs)
- Before a new test takes learners' time, measure the error of the cheapest existing alternative, such as predicting from records already held, and show the test beats it head-to-head. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#beat-what-the-records-already-predict)
- Enforce in code the limits on what a score may claim, so no unvalidated score or prediction of a real test appears until evidence supports it. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#block-unsupported-scores-in-code)
- Calibrate a judge on the inputs it actually receives and on cases only judgment can settle; cases a rule already decides test the rules, not the judge. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#calibrate-a-judge-on-what-reaches-it)
- Calibrate a judge against people before tuning a generator on its scores, and if calibration fails, repair the judge or its rubric rather than tune against it. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#calibrate-the-judge-before-tuning-against-it)
- Test a check that decides whether to release help with matched cases that differ only in context, so a check that always withholds or always releases help cannot pass. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#cases-that-differ-only-in-context)
- Before building on labels a model made, have a stratified sample re-graded independently and require high agreement, above all on the in-between classes. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#check-labels-a-model-made)
- Before believing a score, high or low, check the harness, scorer and items that produced it, and when results look uniform or mostly flagged, suspect the check before the content. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#check-the-instrument-before-the-score)
- Don't predict the gain of stacked methods or repeated attempts by multiplying their odds as if they were independent; failures cluster, so measure the combination. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#combined-gains-dont-multiply)
- Compare the overlap between two runs' failures with chance before reading it as a pattern, because when both runs fail most items many shared failures are forced. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#compare-failure-overlaps-with-chance)
- Compare a reliability figure only with the same kind of reliability measured under like conditions, because one test's marginal, test-retest and sub-score reliabilities can differ markedly. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#compare-like-reliability-with-like)
- Compare runs only on the same frozen items, mix and checks, or else type by type, because a shift in mix or difficulty moves the numbers by itself. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#compare-on-the-same-frozen-mix)
- Judge whether one measure, such as a model marker, can replace another by how far and which way individual scores differ, not by correlation, which misses a uniformly generous marker. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#correlation-is-not-agreement)
- Before trusting a check that depends on reference data, count how often that data exists. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#count-the-data-a-check-depends-on)
- Compute an outcome rate over the learners actually enrolled, from one consistent measure, and follow the same learners over time. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#count-the-learners-who-were-there)
- Count banks and rates by distinct, independently certified items per type, not rows, which hide repeats, treating an item's variants as that item and keeping them in one split. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#count-unique-certified-items)
- Count what was checked, not what was filled: a populated answer, score or 'verified' field says nothing about whether the content is right or whether anyone reviewed it. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#count-what-was-checked-not-what-was-filled)
- Base decisions on the first unaided check and let a high-stakes score count only unaided work, because doing something with cues is not doing it unaided. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#decide-on-unaided-work)
- When validating a new measure against an established test, don't let it act as practice for that test. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#dont-rehearse-the-test-you-validate-against)
- When few students take each item, set item parameters from expert defaults and choose items by simple rules, because a statistically optimal method running on uncalibrated items does no better. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#expert-defaults-before-calibration-data)
- When gating a marker, fail it for passing a wrong or partial answer, and send a right answer it holds back to review rather than failing the gate. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#fail-false-passes-review-held-back-answers)
- Judge whether an item leaks from how its stem, options and key relate to the passage, not from topic words in the passage, which barely separate leaky from clean items. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#find-leaks-in-the-item-not-the-topic)
- Before comparing transcripts with scripts, fix which differences count as the same spoken form, such as numerals, contractions and spelling variants, and send names and dialect to a person. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#fix-spoken-form-equivalences-before-comparing)
- Fix the scoring rule and the expected answers before measuring, apply them the same way to every option, and leave arguable cases out. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#fix-the-rubric-first)
- Find the ability range over which a test measures reliably, flag every score outside it, and send students below its floor to an instrument built for them. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#flag-scores-outside-the-reliable-range)
- Judge any gain against the growth that students with the same starting score, grade and weeks of instruction make anyway, which is small for stronger students in later grades. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#frame-a-gain-against-normal-growth)
- When comparing continuous scores, test a new way of generating against a freshly generated baseline, not stored outputs re-scored, because generation varies between runs even at temperature zero. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#generate-the-baseline-afresh)
- Tie every piece of work to its student, content and attempt, and show results openly, poor ones included. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#hide-no-poor-results)
- When items share a prompt or source, compute confidence intervals over those clusters, since repeated material makes a sample look more precise than it is. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#intervals-over-clusters-of-shared-material)
- Judge a diagnostic by whether students who follow its routing learn faster, and stop testing once the next item is worth less than the study time it takes. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#judge-a-diagnostic-by-its-routing)
- Judge a writing aid, such as a specification or prompt, by the items it leads to, against a no-aid baseline on the same requests, not by how it reads. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#judge-a-writing-aid-by-its-items)
- Judge material by the time students need to reach a verified outcome, not by time spent, which measures presence rather than learning. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#judge-by-hours-to-a-verified-outcome)
- Check a marker's feedback text on its own, apart from its verdicts: every verdict can be right while the feedback misreads the text or the student's answer. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#judge-the-feedback-apart-from-the-verdict)
- Passing items don't make a good set, lesson or course, so judge each whole on how its parts work together, taking item verdicts as given, never by adding them up. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#judge-the-lesson-not-only-its-items)
- Use an ability estimate that stays finite for all-right and all-wrong answers without pulling the strongest and weakest students toward the average, and test it on extreme answer patterns. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#keep-ability-estimates-finite-at-the-extremes)
- Keep the checks from becoming the target: attack the judge with a deliberate cheater, rotate the probes each round, and hold a frozen set back to catch gaming. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#keep-checks-from-becoming-the-target)
- When students sit an adaptive test again, exclude the items they answered recently and cap each item's exposure, letting both rules give way before either could stop a session. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#keep-recent-items-out-of-a-retest)
- Keep expected verdicts, answer keys, flaw names and split labels out of everything a model under test sees, file names and retrieval queries included. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#keep-the-answers-out-of-the-test)
- What was taught together tends to be known or missing together, so in a course with few prerequisites, sample a diagnostic by teaching unit rather than testing every component. [record](groups/how-people-learn.md#knowledge-taught-together-moves-together)
- Log what learners did as raw events and compute any model's reading of them later, so the record doesn't change when the model does. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#log-what-learners-did-not-inferences)
- Measure a comprehension conversation by the turns it runs, how much language the learner produces and whether each reply used the learner's answer, not by responses per minute alone. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#measure-a-conversation-by-turns-and-uptake)
- Measure and headline a generator on one attempt per input fixed in advance, per item type, in the mode that ships, on untuned topics, reporting retried or best-of-several rates apart. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#measure-a-generator-on-one-try)
- Measure the depth mix of generated questions, such as the share needing reasoning rather than recall, as its own figure against a target, since generators skew and accuracy hides it. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#measure-the-depth-mix-a-writer-produces)
- Name each judged part by what it is rather than by its position, so its judgments can be compared across drafts. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#name-judged-parts-by-content-not-position)
- Don't adapt to a student on fewer than three observations. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#never-adapt-on-one-observation)
- No item format is leak-proof and each leaks its own way, so test every item with the text hidden using a strong solver. [record](groups/writing-questions.md#no-format-is-leak-proof)
- In any record of what a student knows, keep what was never tested apart from what was tested and failed. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#not-tested-is-not-failed)
- Before a marker scores students, probe every marked item with the same set of answers, among them partial, wrong, misspelled, negated and empty ones, and the hint echoed back. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#one-answer-set-for-every-marked-item)
- Count a gain or certify work only on the grader of record in its current version; stand-in judges, older versions and self-evaluation are design evidence only. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#only-the-grader-of-record-confirms-a-gain)
- Before a judged check holds back a lesson or steers a student, score it on cases a person labelled; a model-made label measures nothing. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#person-labelled-cases-first)
- In an adaptive test, pick at random among the few most informative items, not always the single best, or a fixed starting estimate gives every session the same opening. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#pick-at-random-among-the-best)
- Report a pass rate over every requested item from live verdicts only, keeping errored, unrun or cached checks and other pipeline failures apart from content failures, none dropped. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#pipeline-failures-are-not-content-failures)
- Treat placement and gap-finding as different diagnostics: placement in an ordered course needs only a short binary search, while finding gaps needs sampling across the course. [record](groups/how-people-learn.md#placement-and-gap-finding-are-different-diagnostics)
- Plant each check's test faults in the forms writers actually produce, not only the blatant one, since a check that catches its planted form can pass every other. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#plant-faults-in-the-forms-writers-produce)
- Compute a score's precision only from the items the student actually answered, never from artificial items added to steady the estimate. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#precision-only-from-answered-items)
- Call a check delayed only when the time that passed is recorded and proves the delay, because success on the same day shows short-term memory, not learning. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#prove-a-delay-before-claiming-it)
- Before crediting a model with using the rules or material it is given, test it with them removed, shuffled, deliberately wrong and contrary to what it already knows. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#prove-a-model-uses-what-its-given)
- Prove a generator on every item type, scope, grade and difficulty it will serve, accepting it on its worst, since success on one shows nothing about another. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#prove-each-new-scope-on-its-own)
- Qualify each model and version on its own against the rules it will follow, with controls that catch a rule applied outside its scope. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#qualify-every-model-and-version)
- End an adaptive test only when its minimum length, content quotas and precision target are all met, under a hard cap, so meeting the quotas alone never stops it. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#quotas-alone-never-stop-a-test)
- When students took different items or forms, never compare or correlate their raw or percent-correct scores; first equate them, or estimate ability from the answers and each item's difficulty. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#raw-counts-dont-compare-across-item-sets)
- When a planted fault goes uncaught, plant it again in content the check is known to read before changing the rule, because a run that reads nothing passes everything. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#re-plant-a-missed-fault-before-blaming-the-rule)
- Re-run only a check that failed to finish, never one whose verdict was unwelcome, and certify nothing on missing evidence. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#re-run-failures-never-verdicts)
- Count fixes, gains or checks as proven only on fresh held-out items written apart from whoever built them, kept from tuning, training and repair, never rerun to chase a pass. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#re-test-every-claim-on-fresh-items)
- Read why items failed from the judge's structured scores, not by matching keywords in its prose. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#read-failure-causes-from-the-scores)
- Learn and report what a judge or gate actually does from its code and saved verdicts, tested on a few items, not from documentation or a published description. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#read-what-the-evaluator-really-runs)
- Don't carry pass bars, yields or thresholds measured on one course or grade over to another; recalibrate them on a control set there first. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#recalibrate-bars-before-reusing-them)
- Record how long each answer took as well as whether it was right, since a fast wrong answer marks a confident misconception and a slow right one fragile knowledge. [record](groups/how-people-learn.md#record-speed-with-every-answer)
- When people review a generator's output to measure it, let them accept or reject each piece but never repair, add, merge or rescope it, so its omissions stay measurable. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#reject-never-repair-what-is-measured)
- Change a check only after a paired trial on frozen scenarios shows a gain and no new misses, even when just adding reference text, which can make a judge worse. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#replace-a-check-only-on-a-paired-trial)
- When items are served from a bank of pre-checked items, report how many fresh requests the bank covers separately from how good its items are. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#report-a-banks-coverage-apart-from-quality)
- Score a judge on labelled cases per kind of fault, counting misses and false alarms against a baseline that always gives the majority verdict, with its bar from human agreement. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#report-a-judges-catch-rate-per-fault)
- Report how students do skill by skill, on items that need the text. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#report-accuracy-by-skill)
- Report how many learners used the material and how much of the intended work they completed beside any accuracy figure, because accuracy counts only those who finished. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#report-completion-beside-accuracy)
- Report each readiness gate on its own, with how it was verified and what is still open, never averaging gates or letting one stand in for another. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#report-each-readiness-gate-on-its-own)
- Report the mean score beside the pass rate, fixing in advance which decides, because a pass cut turns small shifts near the bar into noise or false gains. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#report-pass-rate-and-mean)
- Report every result as measured on real output, misses included and never rounded across its bar, recomputed from primary records; a projected, templated or summary-copied figure is no result. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#report-the-number-that-was-measured)
- When learners arrive with gaps a course doesn't own, report outcomes both for the cohort without those gaps and for the whole cohort, never only one. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#report-the-ready-and-the-whole-cohort)
- Let a reproducible procedure such as hashing select and split examples, never assign their meaning labels, which need independent annotation from the source, measured agreement and adjudication. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#reproducible-is-not-valid)
- Once items are in use, review each one's statistics as a standing duty, and revise, quarantine or retire those that fail. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#review-each-item-after-real-use)
- Before reading a failed check as a gap in its content, rule out the prerequisite it leans on, such as decoding for reading comprehension or fluency for math. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#rule-out-the-prerequisite-first)
- Before any full run and after every change, run a small fixed smoke set end to end, and use it to find bugs, not to estimate rates. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#run-a-fixed-smoke-set-first)
- To estimate a single-try or best-of-several rate from a stored pool of scored candidates, sample the pool at random or generate afresh. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#sample-a-stored-pool-at-random)
- Test a claim across everything it covers, every lesson, unit, standard, bank and population, not a showcase or convenient slice, reporting the share that holds and what went unchecked. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#sample-every-unit-the-claim-covers)
- Save the first attempt as well as the last: first-try answers are how two designs get compared. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#save-the-first-attempt)
- Label every number and claim with what produced it, its grader, version, attempts, sample, scope and evidence class, and word the claim no more strongly than that evidence covers. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#say-what-produced-every-number)
- Measure growth and link tests on an equal-interval scale score, never on percentile ranks, whose steps are unequal and which move whenever the norm set changes. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#scale-scores-not-percentiles)
- When scoring a judge against labelled cases, count every case it failed to judge or abstained on as a miss unless the case truly can't be decided. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#score-a-judges-abstentions)
- Report accuracy on the parts of a structured answer that vary between items apart from parts fixed for every item, because fixed parts inflate field-level scores and hide real mistakes. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#score-fixed-and-varying-fields-apart)
- Score a narration's fidelity to its source on the script, not a recogniser's transcript, whose errors count as changes, and use recognition only to confirm the audio reads it. [record](groups/pictures-sound-film.md#score-narration-fidelity-on-the-script)
- Read results from learners who happened to be measured, or who chose to do more, as a biased sample that can flatter a course, never as proof it works. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#selected-learners-are-a-biased-sample)
- Treat what a student says they know as a starting guess that their answers can overturn, never as proof of mastery. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#self-report-is-a-prior-not-proof)
- Set a judge's pass threshold in the gap between its lowest-scoring labelled pass and highest-scoring labelled miss, after checking the gap holds across repeated runs. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#set-a-judges-threshold-in-the-gap)
- Set each pass or leak bar above the item's chance score, computed from how the live system scores it, and within what the judge can reliably reach, never near-perfect. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#set-the-bar-the-judge-can-reach)
- Before measuring, write down what each result will mean and the ship, hold and abort bars, count the thing the decision is about, and apply the bars as written. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#set-the-decision-thresholds-first)
- Don't show a clock unless speed is what the task measures, but quietly log how long learners take on each item and flag rushed answers. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#show-no-clock-but-log-the-time)
- Claim a rate or a difference only from a sample sized to show it, with the count reported, and a difference only once it clears the judge's run-to-run spread. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#size-the-sample-to-the-claim)
- Let a score drive only the decisions its reliability supports, holding high-stakes decisions about one student to a stricter bar than screening or instructional ones. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#stakes-set-the-reliability-a-score-needs)
- State every scoring rule once, so the judge's instructions, the rubric's layers, the written specification and the pass rule in code agree. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#state-each-rule-once)
- Put the measurement in place, check it, and take a baseline before changing a course, because a gain with nothing measured before it can't be shown. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#take-a-baseline-before-any-change)
- Test a generator for variety by sending it the same request several times and measuring how far the outputs differ; one whose outputs barely differ is static. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#test-a-generator-for-variety)
- When the items that survive the checks cannot fill the test plan, report a failure, not a pass with named gaps. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#too-few-survivors-is-a-failure)
- Test a generator with several phrasings of each request, since its quality should not depend on how a request is worded. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#vary-the-wording-of-each-request)
- The same marker can give different verdicts on the same answer: send each test answer more than once, and re-send every known miss after each rebuild. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#verdicts-vary)
- Track word knowledge with its own periodic check outside passage questions, because comprehension scores can hold for about two years while a vocabulary gap widens. [record](groups/how-people-learn.md#vocabulary-gaps-show-before-comprehension-falls)
- A percentage mastery bar means something only while the task mix, difficulty and support stay fixed, so set it per skill, not by one global figure. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#calibrate-the-mastery-bar-per-skill)
- In simulation, judge whether a test survives wrong item difficulties by its error against the true abilities, not by its reliability, which can stay flat while that error grows. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#judge-robustness-against-true-ability)
- When testing a marker, label each test answer with every verdict a fair marker could give, such as secure or partial for an arguable answer, rather than one expected verdict. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#label-test-answers-with-every-fair-verdict)
- In an adaptive test, give each student next the most informative item at their current estimate, but within content quotas, because information alone ignores the blueprint. [record](groups/checking-answers.md#most-informative-item-within-content-quotas)

## 7. Improve: fix causes, one change at a time

- A fix a checker proposes changes only the faulty span, keeps the objective and the useful teaching content, and adds no unsupported claim. [record](groups/ai-judges-and-markers.md#a-fix-touches-only-the-fault)
- A failure that repeats across items or retries is a fault, not noise: redrafting or rescoring won't fix it, so rule out broken measurement, find its cause and test it. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#a-repeating-failure-is-a-fault-to-find)
- Map every point of feedback on its own to a decision and its evidence, and never drop one or bury it in a bulk statement that feedback was addressed. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#account-for-every-point-of-feedback)
- Before committing to an overall target, add up each item type's realistic headroom to see whether the target can be reached and which type decides it. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#add-up-the-headroom-first)
- Before making more material, audit how much already exists and how good it is, and ask whether the failure is an absence or a fault in what exists. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#audit-what-exists-before-making-more)
- Before choosing a fix or calling a change a gain, break results down by item type, unit and failure kind, and apply changes only where they help: averages hide losses. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#break-results-down-before-fixing)
- When a delivery format or schema cannot carry approved content, change the format, the schema or the code that renders it, never the content's claims, sources or meaning. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#change-the-format-never-the-content)
- Before acting on a cited result, requirement or analysis of what a test measures, check it against its original source, because summaries merge, move and relabel what they cite. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#check-cited-results-against-their-sources)
- When a request contradicts itself or the course's scope, or different generators fail it the same way, fix or reject the request before changing the generator. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#check-the-request-before-the-generator)
- Before cutting or trimming part of a lesson, check what only it supplies and what later lessons ask of it. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#check-what-a-cut-takes-away)
- Treat each defect, finding or failure as a class: repair its kind and add a standing check across everything made the same way, not just the items that failed. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#close-the-class-not-the-instance)
- Before crediting or blaming a change, confirm in the outputs that it took effect for every item type, since a change that never ran can still show a gain. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#confirm-the-change-actually-fired)
- When a lesson feels long, cut its redundant repeats, such as an answer shown twice, before any content, but keep deliberate returns like a framing line. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#cut-repeats-before-content)
- Settle the decisions that change what content must be, such as its target level, before regenerating anything, then fold every fix into one gated regeneration. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#decide-first-then-regenerate-once)
- Don't let a later step or model build on generated output, a model's own research included, until it is checked and approved, since its errors carry forward. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#dont-build-on-unchecked-output)
- When a flagged item is ruled fine, record the ruling where the checker reads it, so the same false flag doesn't come back on later runs. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#feed-every-ruling-back-to-the-checker)
- Treat feedback on one course as a ruling for that course only, and check each point against another course's build before carrying it over. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#feedback-rules-only-its-own-course)
- Name why items fail from what the judge was given and what it said about the failing items. [record](groups/generating-with-a-model.md#find-the-cause-in-the-failing-items)
- When many runs agree on a limit, find what they all had in common before calling it a limit. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#find-what-every-run-shared)
- Before blaming content for low completion, check when learners started, how accurate they were before stopping and how long the course is against the time they had. [record](groups/making-and-reviewing.md#find-why-completion-is-low-first)
